# Mizan – Support

We read every email and reply as soon as we can.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do the prayer times come from?
Mizan shows each mosque's own times – the start (adhan) time and the jamaat time – exactly as the mosque publishes them on its website or timetable. We refresh them every night. We never change or "correct" a mosque's times.

### Why are my mosque's times different from another app?
Many apps calculate prayer times from the position of the sun. Mizan shows what your mosque itself publishes, so the times can differ by a few minutes – your mosque's times are the ones it actually prays by.

### My mosque shows "city times" or no jamaat. Why?
Some mosques don't publish their times online, or publish them in a form we can't read yet. When that happens, Mizan shows calculated times for your area and tells you so. If you have your mosque's timetable, tap **"Have their full timetable? Send it to us"** on the Calendar screen or email it to us.

### I spotted a wrong time.
Please tap **"Report a mistake"** in the app, or email us with the mosque's name, the date and the prayer. We'll check it against the mosque's own timetable.

### My mosque isn't in the app.
Email us its name, address and website (if it has one) and we'll look into adding it.

### I'm not getting prayer reminders.
- Check that notifications are turned on for Mizan: iPhone **Settings → Notifications → Mizan**.
- Check that a Focus mode (for example Do Not Disturb or Sleep) isn't silencing them.
- Open Mizan once a day or so, so it can plan the next reminders.

### Can I use Mizan without sharing my location?
Yes. Choose **"Search by City or Postcode"** in Settings and pick your town or postcode instead.

### Does Mizan work outside the UK?
Yes. Mosque times are available for UK mosques. Everywhere else, Mizan shows calculated prayer times for your location.

### What about my data?
Mizan has no account, no ads and no tracking, and your settings stay on your phone. See our [Privacy Policy](https://al-aar.github.io/mizan-legal/).
