Mizan – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 22 October 2026
Mizan ("the app") shows prayer and jamaat times published by UK mosques. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and your rights. Mizan is run by Muzammil Ahmed, an individual developer based in the UK.
Contact: mizantheapp@gmail.com
The short version
No account. You don't sign up or give us your name, email or phone number to use Mizan.
No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Mizan contains no advertising or analytics tools.
Your location is used only to find your nearest mosques and prayer times. We do not keep a record of where you are.
Your settings stay on your phone.
What the app uses and why
1. Your location (only if you allow it)
If you allow location access, the app uses your phone's position to:
find the mosques nearest to you. Your approximate position is sent to our mosque database (hosted by Supabase in London, UK), which returns the nearest mosques. We do not store your position.
calculate prayer times for your area when a mosque has not published its own times. Your position is sent to the AlAdhan prayer-times service (aladhan.com), which returns the calculated times.
show your town's name. Your phone's built-in service (Apple) turns your position into a place name.
show the Qibla direction. This is worked out on your phone.
You can say no, or turn location off at any time in your phone's Settings. You can still search for a town or postcode instead.
2. Town or postcode searches
If you search for a place, the text you type is sent to postcodes.io (UK postcodes) or OpenStreetMap Nominatim (town names) to find its location. These services are not run by us and have their own privacy policies.
3. Settings stored on your phone
Your chosen mosque, location, notification choices and display preferences are saved only on your phone. They are deleted if you delete the app.
4. Notifications
Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not run a notification server and do not collect a notification token.
5. Reporting a mistake or sending a timetable
If you tap "Report a mistake" or "Send us their timetable", your phone's own email app opens with a message to mizantheapp@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply and to correct mosque times, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.
6. Technical logs
Like most online services, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (for example, IP address and time of request) for security and to fix problems. These are deleted automatically after a short period. We do not use them to identify you.
7. Beta testing (TestFlight)
If you test Mizan through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, basic usage information and any feedback you send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.
Legal basis (UK GDPR)
Location: your consent, given through your phone's permission prompt. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
Emails you send us and technical logs: our legitimate interest in providing accurate prayer times, replying to you and keeping the service secure.
Who we share information with
Only the services listed above, so that the app can work: Supabase (database hosting), AlAdhan (prayer-time calculation), postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search), Apple (place names, TestFlight), and Google (our Gmail inbox). We never sell your information.
Children
Mizan is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Your rights
Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email mizantheapp@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
